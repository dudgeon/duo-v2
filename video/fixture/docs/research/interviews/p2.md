- Date: Sep 15, 2026
- Box: Elm Park box
- Who: retired teacher, steward

## Context
Gerald taught for thirty-one years and stewards the Elm Park box. He keeps a paper list of what is in it and compares the app against it.

## What they did
- Scanned the Elm Park box in the morning, in good light.
- Checked the result against his paper list, line by line.
- Counted two wrong books out of fourteen and corrected them by hand in the catalog.

## In their words
> “I check every one. Old habit. Two of them were just wrong.”
> “In the afternoon the door catches the sun and it’s hopeless.”

## Observations

- Noticed glare on the door before we did.
