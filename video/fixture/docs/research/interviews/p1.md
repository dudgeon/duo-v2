# P1 Aisha

- Date: Sep 14, 2026
- Box: Northgate Station box
- Who: commuter, scans on the way to the train
- Interviewer: Sam

## Context
Aisha passes the Northgate Station box twice a day on her commute and scans it standing on the platform, one hand on her bag. She has scanned about thirty shelves since August.

## What they did
- Opened the app on the platform and pointed it at the top two shelves.
- Watched the list fill in and tapped Save before her train came.
- Showed us a shelf where three of fourteen books were wrong.

## In their words
> “I scan it on the way to the train. If it showed me the list first I’d tap through it right there.”
> “I don’t mind fixing one or two. I mind not knowing which ones to fix.”

## Observations
- Scans in under a minute, mostly one-handed.
- Wants the unsure books flagged rather than checking every spine.
- Took part in the Oct 12 review prototype test.
