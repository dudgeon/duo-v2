# Reference analysis

Geoff chose two references on 2026-10-10:
- **Paper Mono**: Paper's launch film for the typeface. Vlad Moroz, 1:00, 1080².
- **Loom, "Create a video prompt for agents"**: Vicki Heart, 0:29, 1920×1080.

Both were downloaded for private study into `~/Library/Caches/duo-video/refs/`. They are never committed and never used in the video. Contact sheets were taken at 2 frames per second.

We take their **grammar**, never their content: no logos, no copy, no imagery, and not the Paper Mono font.

## What they share

| | Paper Mono | Loom | What we take |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | Warm off-white paper (≈ #F4F2EC) with a faint graph grid; ASCII-art clouds in pale blue drift behind | White with a faint graph grid; a blurred real desktop only at the open and close; a pastel lilac-to-peach wash for the reveal | **Light paper ground with a faint grid** for graphics beats; the **real desktop (High Sierra) only for the open and close** |
| The UI | Small floating windows: 1 px thin blue or black border, a mono title bar (`o.glyph  ×`), square corners, no shadow or a hairline one | The real product UI, full-bleed or as one floating panel; at the end, windows stack in a 3D cascade | **Real Duo captures as floating panels.** Mini windows with mono title bars carry overlays |
| Type | Mono everywhere, very large; the same word repeated with a weight ramp; outlined, numbered chapter titles (`1 Design`) | One clean sans, left-aligned, two lines. The second line arrives grey, then turns black (`Talk through it. / Show what matters.`) | **SF Pro headlines, left-aligned, two-tone reveal; SF Mono for labels and title bars.** No centred title on a gradient |
| Colour | One blue accent (≈ #5B8DEF) and one warm second (ochre ≈ #D9A441); one inverted black section | Near-monochrome; Loom blue for annotations; one red record dot | **Paper and ink, Duo's needs-you orange as the one accent**, blue for annotations |
| Pointing at things | A real cursor drives every change; things happen where it clicks | Hand-drawn blue marker circles and strokes on the UI; the narration quoted in tiny type above (`"The header should be shorter."`) | **A real cursor + a marker stroke that draws itself + the spoken words quoted small** |
| Motion | Things snap open (windows pop with no fade); progress bars count; type is retyped live; cuts on the beat | Calm push-ins; windows slide and settle; the toolbar morphs (button to timer to dots); it loops back to the first frame | **UI snaps, panels settle, camera glides**; one shape that morphs; the end frame echoes the first |
| Depth | 3D magazine pages curl in perspective with real shadow | A 3D cascade of 3–4 tilted windows on the pastel wash | **One perspective cascade** for "many projects" (S2) and the final pull-back |
| Pace | A new thing every 0.5–1 s in the busy parts; holds about 2 s on a finished frame | Holds 1–2 s per idea, a calmer edit | **2–3 s per idea, on the voice's words** (we're narrated, so slower than Paper) |

## Banned defaults

Any of these fails the critique:
- A centred title on a gradient.
- Everything fading in.
- Rounded pill labels.
- Soft spotlight dimming.
- Glow rings.
- Generic particles.
- Frames or text crammed in the corners.
- An app screenshot just sitting there with nothing moving or pointing.

## The overlay language

Each callout replaces the earlier ring, spotlight and lift. It has three parts, timed to the line:
1. **The cursor** travels to the feature, on a spring, and clicks: a tiny press.
2. **A marker stroke** draws itself around the feature: a hand-drawn ellipse, 3 px, annotation blue, slightly open where it ends. It draws over about 0.45 s.
3. **A mini window** snaps open beside it.
   - **Title bar:** mono, the feature's name (`needs-you · 3`), with `×`.
   - **Body:** the feature's own pixels at 2× (a real crop from the 4x capture) or one fact in mono.
   - **Caption:** the narrated phrase quoted small above it (`"waiting on you"`).

## Headlines

- **Placement:** left-aligned at the safe margin, on paper.
- **Reveal:** line 1 ink, line 2 arrives in `text2` grey and turns ink as the voice reaches it.
- **Size:** 96–120 px SF Pro semibold, tracking −2.
- **Never** centred, never on a gradient.
