P3 · 1 · Comments on slides (Q-395, Q-396)
For approval. DL-284: comments are Review in Markdown's, in the deck's source. This board decides how slides show them and how you add one from a slide. Every mark [P]; no new colour or token.
1A pin where a comment is. A comment on a slot (written on its own line in that slot, DL-284) puts a pin at the slot box's top right: an 18-high capsule on pane, a 1 pt controlEdge edge, a stroke speech mark and the count in text 10/16 semibold. A slide comment puts it at the slide's top right. Hovering a pin outlines its slot as Select Shape does (1 pt dashed text, with the name tag).
2Click a pin and the thread opens in the source's margin (R11), scrolled to and made active, as DL-237 draws it, and the slides keep the keyboard. Folded to the slides, it opens as DL-237's narrow 280 popover over the slides instead.
3Add Comment from a slide (R12): Select Shape, pick a slot, and the picked bar (DL-125 C) gains Add Comment first. It opens the margin's composer on that slot's lines without taking the keyboard from the slides. Duo writes the signed comment on its own line at the end of the slot. A slide's blank area picks the whole slide. ⌥⌘M does the same on a picked shape.
4The source shows the comment as it does in any Markdown file: highlighted text, the card in the margin. The signature line is drawn by Review in Markdown, not by Duo Decks. Closed comments leave no pin (DL-237).
Rejected: a comment rail beside the slides (it duplicates the margin in a 470 side); a count by the slide number only (it doesn't say which element).Verbs: duo2 deck comments (threads by slide and slot), duo2 deck comment <slide>[/<slot>] "text"; pins are drawn from the provider's comments(rev) event.
