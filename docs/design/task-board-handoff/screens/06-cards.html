Anatomy [P]

  • Card: pane on the lane’s ground, 1 rule border, radius 6 (radiusCard), padding 8 10 9; lanes 6 apart. Width follows the lane (180 to 240).
  • Line 1: the task box (TaskBox, as a TaskLine) and the title, semibold 13/18, wrapping to 3 lines then truncating.
  • Line 2, 12/16 text2, indented to the title: waiting on …, owner (when not you), due (calendar mark), done …. Missing fields leave no gap.
  • Line 3: sessions, from the note’s sessions: list (DL-93). The most urgent one leads with its name and wait (line 2 says how many when there are several); idle-only shows glyphs and a count.
  • Not shown: tags, milestone, done_when, depends_on (ENH-36 lets a user pick extra properties, as Bases does).
