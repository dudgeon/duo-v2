3 · Also to choose

Where needs-you sessions go. (a) Keep NEEDS YOU on top, above both groups, as drawn Recommended. A waiting session leaves its task or time group until you answer, which keeps attention first. (b) No NEEDS YOU section. The hand marks the session where it sits, and its task or group header shows the hand with a count and sorts first. That is quieter, but a closed fold can hide it.

Order. Tasks first, as in your note Recommended, or Other sessions first, since that’s where a new session lands.

Look. Task headers keep their chevron, box and status. Their glyph slot shows the hand while one of their sessions needs you (here “Pricing copy review”, up in NEEDS YOU, belongs to Launch email), and is empty otherwise. Time groups are fold rows (a chevron, the name in semibold, and a count). Both sit at the same level. A task’s sessions hang under the thread rule, and a time group’s sessions are indented to match. Each category header shows a + on hover: New task, or New Claude Code session.
