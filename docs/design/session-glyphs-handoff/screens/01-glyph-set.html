A · The recommended set, in every state

Each glyph is 9 pt in a 10 × 10 box, with round caps and joins. Colours come from tokens only. Shape carries every state: outline means quiet, a cursor or dots mean working, solid means done and unseen, the hand means needs you, and the tick means resolved. Nothing animates (README, Motion). Hovering a session row changes nothing today, and that stays.
