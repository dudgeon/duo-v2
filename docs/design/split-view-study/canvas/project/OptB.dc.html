B · Two tab groups
The right pane holds up to two groups, each with its own strip, like an editor’s “split right”. Legacy deferred this as its Option B. Every mark [P].
1Each side has its own strip. The pane rule sits over the active group’s strip. ⌃Tab cycles within that group.
2Two strips at about 358 each already truncate at 1440 with three tabs. A deck’s Slides is a tab of its own in the other group, so the source and its slides can drift apart when you switch tabs.
3Make one: drag a tab to the other side, Move to Other Side, Open Beside. Any item can go in either group.
Serves: deck ✓ · Markdown + preview ✓ · HTML + page ✓ · two documents ✓ · more than one pair at once ✓. Narrow: a group folds away and its tabs go where?: a new rule to design (merge into the other group, or hide). Legacy’s issues: reopens the focus problem twice over (group and tab), plus drop zones and two sets of tab rules (DL-225’s scrolling, Q-210’s pinned tabs) per group. Cost: about 12–15 days.
