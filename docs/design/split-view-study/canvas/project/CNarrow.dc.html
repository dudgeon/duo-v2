One document. Sidebar 300 · console 518 · right pane 460, as DL-129 draws a 1280 window.
Preview Beside. The sidebar folds away, the console narrows to its 480 minimum, and the right pane takes the rest: 799, two sides of 399. Same motion as a pane hiding (motionPaneToggle, 200 ms; the terminal is resized once, at the end).
Peek. ⌃⌘S or the sidebar button slides the sidebar over the console. The split doesn’t move. Picking a session or a file, Esc or a click outside closes it.
Close the split. Duo gives back exactly what it took: the sidebar and the console return to the widths you had.
When does the sidebar fold? When sidebar + console 480 + two sides of 340 don’t fit: under about 1,464 pt with a 300 sidebar. So it folds at 1440 and 1280. On a wider window (a 1728 or 1920 display) it stays. The console always narrows to 480 while a split shows: split view is for focused work on the document. Why it’s acceptable: a split is focused work on a document. The sidebar is a click (or ⌃⌘S) away and never reflows the split. Still too narrow (you dragged things narrower): the split folds to the active side, and the split tab’s two names become a toggle. Nothing squeezes under 340.
