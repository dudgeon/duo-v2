C · Split tab: a deck’s source beside its slides
Recommended. 1440 × 900: the sidebar folds away and the console narrows to 480 (board 8, Geoff’s rule), so the right pane is 959: two sides of 479. Every mark [P].
1One tab, two sides. The strip shows the split as one tab, its two names joined by the split mark. The active side’s name is semibold. A file stays in one place, ⌃Tab steps over the split as one tab, and the pair closes, drags and restores together.
2The active-pane rule (DL-165) spans only the active side. It moves to the slides when you click them. Nothing else changes and nothing moves.
3The formatting bar and its mic (DL-266) sit over the source side only, and only while it has the keyboard. Dictation goes into the source.
4The caret in slide 3’s lines makes slide 3 current. A quiet 2 pt controlEdge mark in the margin shows which lines belong to it. Clicking text on a slide puts the caret in its slot. Scroll-follow is off by default (Duo Decks R3).
5The preview keeps its own bar: ‹ › n/N, problems, Select Shape, Export ⌄ and the export state (R6). Under 460 the deck bar already reads 3/10. Split view draws no bar of its own.
6The slides redraw about 250 ms after typing pauses, from the unsaved buffer: the new deck is drawn first, then swapped in, keeping the scroll position (R1, R2). Rendering never writes a file.
Make it: the deck opens split when there’s room (Q-367). For any file with a preview: Preview Beside in View, on the tab’s menu, or ⌥⌘P (a new chord, Q-364). Close: the hover × on a name closes that side, and ⌘W closes the active side. The other side stays as an ordinary tab. Separate makes two tabs. Divider: drag it; double-click evens it. Each side’s minimum is 340 (Q-366). The ratio is kept with the split. Cost: slice 1 (pairs: deck, Markdown, HTML) about 5 days, on today’s single viewers. Slice 2 (doc beside doc) about 5 more.
