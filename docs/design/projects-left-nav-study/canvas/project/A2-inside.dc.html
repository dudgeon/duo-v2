What the row and the list do

P‹ Projects, a 24-high row above the header, in text2; when sessions in other projects need you it says so in needsYou (“2 elsewhere”), the same count as the peek's (⇧⌘P).

PThe list slides in from the left over the sidebar, motionPaneToggle 200 ms (instant with Reduce Motion). The project you're in is open and selected. A click on another project opens it; Done, Esc or a click on this project slides back.

P⇧⌘O opens the list with its filter focused, at either altitude (Q-530). Matches show their folder; ↩ opens the first. A folder in Home that isn't a project offers Make a Project.

PThe sidebar button and ⌃⌘S keep their meaning: show or hide the left pane. Hidden, the list can still be opened with ⇧⌘O, which shows the pane.
