Spec [P]

Where. The pane's foot, 36 high on ground, a rule above. Shown while webFocus == .editor on a Markdown document. It goes when the keyboard leaves (⌥⌘←, a click in the console), but not while one of its own menus is open. No slide (it comes with focus); Reduce Motion is moot.

Controls. The same set and look as A. Clicks never take focus, so the bar can't hide itself mid-click.

Tables. Unchanged (DL-113): the in-document bar, and Format › Table.

Mic. 26 round at the bottom right, chat's spot. At rest it's only there while you edit, so nothing shows that dictation is possible until you're in the document. That's what Geoff asked the voice round to fix in the terminal.

duo2 (DL-71). doc format body|quote|bullet|numbered|codeblock, voice start --doc. There's no show/hide verb, because focus drives it.

Build, about 4½ days. 3 shared. 1 for the foot bar and its focus binding. ½ to keep the caret in view when the web view loses 36 at the foot. Amends nothing.
