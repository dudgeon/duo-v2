What changes from DL-266 / DL-301 [P]

Added to the bar: Comment (⌥⌘M; with no selection, the word at the caret) and Suggest Edits (⇧⌘E; a dot that fills when on, “Suggest” beside it when there's room). These are always there while the bar shows. When the document has suggestions or comments, ‹ › and Review ⌄ join at the right, before the mic.

Review ⌄ holds the Markup modes, Show Closed Comments (with its count), Accept All and Reject All (and per person when there's more than one), then the counts. Under 420 it also holds Next and Previous Change.

Retired: the 44-high review bar (MarkdownReviewBar): amends DL-237's bar and Q-265. Its controls are all here or in Review ⌄. Every menu item and chord stays.

When it shows

While the document has the keyboard, as DL-266.

And whenever the document has suggestions or comments, or Suggest Edits is on, even when you're elsewhere: the old review bar's rule. A plain document you're not editing still shows no bar.

It's laid over the editor as built: 24 high, the top padding 24, a 24 scroll margin, so the text never moves. At rest a reviewed document's bar is just the one 24 strip, where today it's a 44 bar.

Fit

460: writing marks, Comment, Suggest with its word, ‹ › and Review ⌄ (Insert Table into ⋯ when review controls show), the mic. 360: Suggest as its dot, ‹ › into Review ⌄. In a table: Suggest as its dot.
