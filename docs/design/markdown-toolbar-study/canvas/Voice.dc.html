Keys

⇧⌘D goes to the document when it has the keyboard, ahead of the session. A tap toggles; held past 0.3 s it's hold-to-talk (DL-261).

Esc ends and keeps the words, as everywhere outside chat's composer (DL-263).

Return finishes, lets the last phrase land, then is the editor's own Return: a new line, continuing a list. It never sends (Q-346).

Typing or clicking in the document finishes the dictation first, as in chat.
