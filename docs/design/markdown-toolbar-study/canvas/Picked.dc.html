Why 24 [P]

“Much slimmer” than 32. 24 is Duo's existing small row (rowFile, rowTileSession, the tab hover fill size.tabHover). It holds 20-high controls with 2 to spare. 20 is the smallest Duo uses for a click target (the chat strip's toggle is 18 × 14, so 20 isn't new), and it keeps 12/16 labels unclipped. Lower would mean 16-high marks, below the strip's own text.

New token formatBarHeight 24. Controls: glyphs 24 × 20, radius 5, marks 13 in text2; on, or under the pointer, selected with text. Style ⌄ and table buttons are 12/16, 20 high. Mic 20 round, glyph 12. Separators 12 high. 22 in from the left, so Style's word lines up with the text (28).

Overlay, not push-down
Proposed: over the top padding
Not chosen: pushes the text 24

A bar that pushed the document down would move every line 24 each time you clicked in and out. Laid over the editor, it lands in the top padding at the top (padding 22 → 24, the only change to the document). Further down, text scrolls under it, with a 24 scroll margin so nothing you're working on hides.

When it shows

While a Markdown document has the keyboard (webFocus == .editor, the pane's focus rule on): a click in the text, ⌥⌘→ into the pane, a new document opening. It stays while one of its menus is open and while dictating. It goes when the keyboard leaves (the console, the sidebar, another tab), and on read-only documents, a template's preview and Source mode. No motion: it's there at once and gone at once, like the hover ×. Nothing moves, so there's nothing to animate.

At rest nothing shows that a document takes dictation. ⇧⌘D goes to the document once it has the keyboard (before then, to the session as today), and Edit › Start Voice Input says so. Everything on the dictation board holds.
