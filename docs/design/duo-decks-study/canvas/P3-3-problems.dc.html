P3 · 3 · Problems, notes moves and Export in the deck bar
For approval of the look. split-deck already places "problems" and Export ⌄ in the deck's 44 bar (R5, R6); this draws them. Every mark [P]; no new colour.
1Problems ⌄ after the slide count, only when there are any: a warning triangle in text and "1 problem" semibold. Its menu lists each problem (slide, slot, message); choosing one reveals both sides. Notices (an empty slot) are counted in the menu, not in the bar.
2Export state to the left of Select Shape in text2: "Changed since export", "Exported 5 m ago" (the time as the console writes it), nothing if never exported.
3Export ⌄: Export as PowerPoint… (writes <deck> YYYY-MM-DD HHmm.pptx, DL-282, removing the previous one, DL-286), Export Again (named the file it replaces), Show Export in Finder; Upload to Google Slides… dimmed until Duo's Google sign-in (ENH-307). After an export, the DL-132 notice: "Exported field-trial-review 2026-11-01 0915.pptx · 10 slides · Show in Finder · Open With ⌄".
4A problem under its slide: a card on pane with the message (slot name semibold) and buttons. A fix with one right answer is a button ("Use kpi" for a typo, DL-298); otherwise Go to Line and Send to Claude. The source line gets diffDelFill and the message under it in diffDelText 11/16, the editor's existing problem colours.
5Content moved to the notes (DL-281) gets the same card under its slide: what moved and why, then Move to left / Move to right (one-answer fixes, DL-298), Split with Claude, Undo Change. It goes when the notes line is gone.
