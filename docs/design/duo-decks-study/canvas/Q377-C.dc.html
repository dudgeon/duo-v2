C · One "Duo Decks" menu on a pptx The menu sits in the viewer's bar and on right-click in Files. It holds the four things you can do with an existing deck:
  • Make This Slide a Template…: the slide on screen, straight to Claude.
  • Make Templates from Slides…: A's picking mode.
  • Convert to Duo Deck…: M3b.
  • New Template Pack from This Deck…: every distinct layout, coalesced (Q-398).
The commonest case, one slide, is two clicks. The rest are discoverable in one place, and nothing new is added to the bar but a menu. Against: a menu hides the picking. "Duo Decks" as a menu title names the feature, not the action. It's a superset of A, so it's more to build at once (it can arrive in steps).
