P3 · 2 · The slide chip, ⌃⌘N and the + gallery (DL-295, DL-296)
For approval of the look; the behaviour is decided. Nobody types <!-- slide: … --> (DL-281). Every mark [P].
1The slide chip (DL-296): in a type: deck file the editor draws each marker line as a 22-high chip with a 1 pt rule above it: "Slide 4 · two-column ⌄" in 11/20, the number semibold in text, the rest text2. The comment text is hidden, the file unchanged. Clicking the chip opens the picker to change the slide's template (DL-285 carries content over; leftovers to the notes, DL-281).
2⌃⌘N, New Slide (DL-295): a new chip appears under the slide the caret is in, with the picker at it. Typing filters (here "k"); each row is the template's thumbnail, its name in mono and its purpose in text2; ↩ inserts the skeleton (title and slot headings, caret in the title), Esc removes the empty chip. The list looks like the editor's autocomplete (.cm-tooltip-autocomplete).
3+ between slides on the slides side: hovering the gap between two slides shows an 18 round + on pane; clicking it opens a gallery of the pack's thumbnails ("Insert a slide after 4"), and choosing one inserts its skeleton at that point in the source, which scrolls to it.
4Same list, three ways in: the chip, ⌃⌘N and the +. Also New Slide… in the editor's context menu. Verb: duo2 deck insert <deck> <template> [--after n]; changing: duo2 deck change <deck> <n> <template>.
