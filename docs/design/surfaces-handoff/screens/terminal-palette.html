The terminal’s colours
Sixteen colours for every terminal in Duo, on the console background with console text as the default. Mid-saturation, so they sit with the two-tone look, and every one reads at 4.5:1 or better except Black.
No orange. Red leans pink and yellow leans lemon, so nothing here can be mistaken for the needs-you accent.
