G · Open above tasks: what OPEN holds

Your note (2026-10-19): “open sessions should be above tasks”. So B becomes PINNED · NEEDS YOU · OPEN · TASKS · OTHER SESSIONS (Today, This week, Earlier) · Archived. The open question is whether an open tab that belongs to a task moves up too. Three ways, same project, same moment. [P] throughout.

O1 · Every open tab

Every tab open in Duo, task or not, in OPEN, most recent first. A task's tab names its task after the title in `text2`, and leaves the task while it's open, as a waiting session leaves it for NEEDS YOU. The task row still counts it.

O2 · Only sessions in no taskChosen

OPEN is today's Open group, lifted above TASKS. A task's tabs stay under their task, which ranks first in TASKS because it has a live session. A task's sessions are only ever under it (DL-174).

O3 · Whole tasks with an open tab

A task with a tab open moves into OPEN with all its sessions, beside the open sessions in no task (board C's NOW). Tasks and sessions share a section again.

Why O1 was recommended (you chose O2)
  • It's what you asked: everything you have open is above every task.
  • It works the way NEEDS YOU and PINNED already do: a session moves up while it's live and goes back when it isn't, and its task row keeps the count.
  • OPEN stays a list of sessions. It doesn't mix in task rows.
Costs
  • A task's sessions are split while one is open. The task name on the row says where it lives.
  • Closing or parking a tab moves the row down. DL-130's row move and the hold while the pointer is in the list soften it.
The same in every option
  • OPEN means a tab open in Duo, running or parked, as Open does today. Sessions working outside Duo (background workers) aren't open. They stay under their task, and that task ranks first.
  • Pinned and needs-you sessions are never repeated in OPEN.
  • OTHER SESSIONS keeps Today, This week and Earlier. Its Open group is gone.
  • OPEN's + on hover is New Claude Code session (moved from OTHER SESSIONS).
  • No open tabs, no OPEN section.
