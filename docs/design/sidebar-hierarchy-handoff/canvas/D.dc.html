The board is where you plan. Dragging a card to In progress, or starting a session on it, brings it back into the sidebar.
Good
  • The shortest sidebar at any scale: the director, what needs you, what's in play.
  • Gives the board a job: the long view lives where columns and status already are.
Costs
  • A long-range task is a click and a view change away, and drag-a-session-onto-a-task (DL-147) can't reach it from the sidebar.
  • Two places list tasks with different rules; people may not trust the sidebar to be complete.
  • Leans on the board, which isn't fully built yet (DL-158).
