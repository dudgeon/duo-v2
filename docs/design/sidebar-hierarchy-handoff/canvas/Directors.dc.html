E · The director's slot, its states, and two directors

Drawn in B's PINNED; A, C and D use the same slot without the label. Only the top of the pane is drawn; the rest is as on B. [P] throughout.

1 · Working

The usual case. Its glyph is its state; the second line counts its running workers (from `duo2 director status`), and adds “1 needs you” when a worker is waiting on you.

2 · It needs you

It stays in its slot with the hand, and its second line says so with its wait. It isn't repeated in NEEDS YOU, whose label counts the rows under it. The project's tile, the chip and the peek count it, as for any session. Where you talk to the director doesn't move.

3 · Not open

No tab, or a parked one (only if Keep Running is off). It keeps its slot, idle, with its last wait. A click opens it and it resumes (DL-156), as any session. The slot is how you find it again.

4 · A replaced director still open

Your idea: both in the slot, the current one first. The old one is greyed (`text2` name), says it was replaced and when, and offers Close (Close Tab: its Claude process ends, the session stays in the list and in Earlier). Once closed it leaves the slot.

How two happen, and what stops them
  • The registry keeps one. .duo/director.json names one current director. duo2 director start where one exists needs --take-over, which retires the old one (DL-200). So a project can't have two directors on record. Nothing to add there.
  • Two can still be open: after a take-over the old session keeps running until it next wakes and stands down; or a resume gave the director a new session id and the old tab is still there. That's drawn in 4.
  • A session named like a director but not on record is an ordinary session. Duo never guesses from a name.
  • Home's director (DL-197) sits in Home's slot, never in a project's.
  • No director: no slot. PINNED holds only Keep Running sessions, and doesn't show at all with none.
Not recommended
  • Show the old one as an ordinary row: quiet, but it's the case you'd want to notice.
  • Hide it: a running Claude process you can't see.
  • A lock that refuses a second open director tab: there's no second director to refuse; the leftover is a session, and closing it is your call.
