What's drawn
1
PINNED · n, a section label like the others, first in the pane. It shows only when there's a director or a Keep Running session; otherwise the pane starts at NEEDS YOU as today.
2
The director, first in PINNED, in its two-line slot: glyph, name in `bodyEmphasis`, pin, wait; then “Director · 2 workers” in `control` `text2`. It's there whether or not it's set to Keep Running.
3
Keep Running sessions follow, as ordinary session rows with their pin, most recent first. A pinned session in a task shows here, not under its task; the task row counts it (“open · 2”, as for a session waiting in NEEDS YOU). Keep Running off sends it back.
4
OPEN · n: tabs open in Duo for sessions in no task, not pinned or waiting on you, most recent first. A task's open tabs stay under their task, which ranks first in TASKS (board G, O2).
6
Later · 11 and the task-line order, as in A.
5
On hover, PINNED's + is Keep Running…: it lists the open sessions not yet kept, and is `duo2 session keep-running`. No new command; Keep Running is already in the session menu and on the tab.
Good
  • One idea, not two: the pin you already see on a tab now also means “stays on top”. No Pin to Top to learn.
  • Long-lived sessions (a director, a monitor, an inbox watcher) are the ones you return to all day. They sit where the eye starts.
  • Works the same in Home and in a project, and with no director at all.
Costs
  • Someone who keeps a session running only to save its start-up gets it on top too. If that turns out to annoy, a separate Pin to Top can come later (ENH).
  • Five or more kept sessions make PINNED long. It shows them all; it's your list.
