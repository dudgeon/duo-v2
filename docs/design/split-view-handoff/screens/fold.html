The window narrows to 1280, Duo’s minimum. The console holds 480; both sides narrow together, to 391. The window alone never folds the split.
You drag the console wider. The two sides narrow together, down to 340 each (Q-366).
Past 340 the split folds. The side you were on fills the pane. The other becomes a 16 pt rail at the pane’s far right, the same convention as the sidebar’s rail. The tab keeps both names, the hidden one dimmed. ⇧⌘[ / ⇧⌘] swap which side shows.
Click the rail (or the dimmed name in the tab): the console narrows just enough for two sides of 340 (here to 580), and the split comes back.

The split’s rail: 16 wide, on pane, a rule on its left. The split mark (11 × 9, text2) sits 10 from the top. The whole rail is one button: selected under the pointer, tooltip “Show Slides in Split View”. duo2 split unfold. When it happens: only when you make the console take the room. Duo’s window is at least 1280 and the sidebar folds to its rail, so it’s a safety net rather than a common state. The two rails, one convention: the sidebar’s rail on the window’s left edge, the split’s rail on the right pane’s right edge. Each brings back what it stands for.
