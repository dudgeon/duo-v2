A row in a project's session list: a session, a thread of forks, a group, or a task.

**Status:** Designed for session, thread and group rows; the open tint and "at prompt"/"working" designed (DL-133); task rows (DL-93) are stand-ins. **Built (DL-174, `session-glyphs-handoff/`):** a session row's glyph is `SessionGlyph` (`>_` or a bubble; working, done and the needs-you hand replace or fill it), a task or group row's glyph slot shows only the hand, and the list's sections are NEEDS YOU, TASKS, OTHER SESSIONS (Open, Today, This week, Earlier), Archived (`Project/SidebarHierarchy.swift`). **In code:** `Project/ProjectPanes.swift` `SidebarRowView`, `SidebarLeafRow`; `Design/SessionGlyph.swift`.

**Anatomy:** session and thread rows are `rowSession` (26): a 10-wide chevron slot (threads) or space, the StateGlyph, the name in `body` (one line, tail truncated), a CountPill for threads, and the wait time (`text2`, never truncated); a session with a tab open in Duo reads `at prompt` or `working` instead and sits on `activeTint`. Group and task rows are `rowGroup` (28): chevron, glyph of their most urgent session, name in `bodyEmphasis`, `group · n` or `task · n` pill, a task's status (past open) in `text2`, wait. Selected: `selected` fill, `radiusSelection`, inset `selectionInset` (8). Open groups and tasks show their sessions under a 1.5 `controlEdge` rule at `threadRuleX`.

**Threads (DL-228, built; `fork-thread-handoff/` `01`, `02`, `05`):** a session and its forks are one item, placed by its liveliest member. Open, the original leads with the chevron down and its forks hang under the thread rule (1.5 `controlEdge`, at `threadRuleX`, or at x 37 inside a task), oldest first; each fork is a plain session row with its own glyph, name and wait, lined up under the original's glyph. Folded, the row keeps the original's name and shows the thread's most urgent member's glyph and wait, with `thread · n`. A click on the row opens the original, the chevron folds the thread (`duo2 view group ‹original id›`). A fork made in Duo opens its thread (`expandedGroups`, per project, nothing on disk); one Duo only finds starts folded. A fork that needs you leaves for NEEDS YOU with "Fork of ‹original›" on a second line (12 on 16, `text2`; the row is 42 high). Section counts count sessions. Stand-in (Q-229): no fork mark on a fork's row.

**Behaviour:** a row opens or focuses that session's console tab (resuming it); a group row opens its group tab; a task row opens its note. Right-click a session: Send to Claude, Find Similar, Copy Link, Make a Task, Add to Task ▸, Move to Project ▸, Archive Session, Delete Session…. Right-click a group: Make a Task; a task: Open Task Note, Status ▸.

**Several selected (DL-247, built; `session-multiselect-handoff/`):** ⇧-click selects from the anchor to the row in the order on screen, ⌘-click adds or removes one row, and neither opens or resumes a session, parked ones included. A plain click opens and clears. Selected rows take `selected` and neighbouring ones join into one block, rounded only at its ends; a row that isn't a session breaks the run. Selection wins over `activeTint`. ⌃-click or right-click on a selected row opens the selection's menu (Make a Task, Add to Task ▸, Move to Project ▸, Archive n Sessions, Unarchive n Sessions, Delete n Sessions…, Park n Sessions), and every selected row takes `menuTarget` while it's open. Esc or a click on empty pane clears; ⌘A selects the list's rows. Delete n Sessions… asks once in a sheet naming each session.

**Motion (DL-130):** Opaque on the pane, so a row travelling past never overprints it. The state glyph swaps in place. With Reduce Motion, it's at once.
