[P] Rules
Arriving eases out, leaving eases in, moving eases in and out. Reduce Motion makes all of Duo’s own motion instant: the change still happens, nothing moves (DL-129). The editor and deck pages follow it through prefers-reduced-motion. Motion shows where something went, that a click worked, or keeps your place. Never for its own sake. Nothing scales, fades or moves over a terminal’s text.
[P] Stays still
State glyphs and the needs-you dot (no pulse, no flash) The hover × on a tab and the hover + on a task: there the moment the pointer is Search results while typing, and the selection moving through them Chat text streaming in, and its scroll pinned to the bottom Switching Terminal ↔ Chat: an instant swap Map order (DL-129); the peek’s rows; the editor’s task box A tile growing into its project: it would blur the terminals
