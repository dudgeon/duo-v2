DECISION 10 What happens to Home Home is one Claude session, shown beside the map at All projects. Window 1 is at All projects; you take window 2 (free, sky tint) up to All projects as well.
A · Home in chat mode where its terminal isn'trecommended
The same rule as decision 6: every window's All projects has Home, its terminal live in one and chat in the rest. ⇧⌘H focuses Home in the window you're in. Pinned windows have no All projects, so ⇧⌘H goes to a free window.
B · A stand-in, Show Here
Home says where it is, and Show Here moves its terminal. Honest, but the dark pane you talk to is empty in every window but one.
C · Home only in the first window
Window 1 is the home window; ⌘↑ and ⇧⌘H anywhere bring it forward. Clear, but window 1 becomes special, which independent windows meant to avoid.
