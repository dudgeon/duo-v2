DECISION 7 A document open in two windows rules.md is open, with unsaved typing, in window 1's editor. You open it in window 2. The same question applies to an HTML page, a deck and a browser tab.
A · Read-only here, Edit Here moves editingrecommended
Window 2 shows the text as window 1 types it, read-only, under a bar. Edit Here hands editing over with the unsaved text, and window 1 goes read-only. One writer, so no merging.
B · Editable in both, kept in step
Type in either; each keystroke reaches the other, and Claude's highlights show in both. What you'd expect, but it's a sync layer of its own, the costliest part of independent windows.
C · Its window comes forward
Opening rules.md in window 2 brings window 1 forward with it. Simple, but you can't keep the document on your second screen while its project is open on the first.
