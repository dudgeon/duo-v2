THE MODEL · GEOFF, 2026-10-07 Independent windows, each optionally pinned to a project and tinted Every window is a whole Duo: both altitudes, any project. ⌥⌘N opens one. A window can be pinned to one project, and given a tint so you can tell it apart at a glance.
Window 1 · not pinned, no tint, at All projects. The refunds tile shows the tint of the window it's open in.
Window 2 · pinned to refunds, sage tint, in front. Its chip counts sessions outside this window.
WHAT FOLLOWS A project, session or document can be in two windows. A terminal and an editor can be live in only one, so boards 6 and 7 decide what the other shows. Pinning (boards 2 and 3) and tints (boards 4 and 5) are new, and each needs its own decisions. duo2 acts on the window that holds the caller's session, then the frontmost window, never by focus alone. Relaunch restores every window: its project, pin, tint and frame.
