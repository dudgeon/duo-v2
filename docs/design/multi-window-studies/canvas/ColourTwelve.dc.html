DECISION 4a′ · Q-441 · SUPERSEDES 4a · ENH-319 · DL-312 Twelve window colours, light and dark [P] Geoff: “we will need more colors; at least 12 … I care less about which specific colors and more that they are cohesive and feel consistent with one another”. One recipe, twelve hues: every colour has the same OKLCH lightness and chroma for each surface, and the hues sit 22.5° apart on an arc from 92° to 339.5° that skips orange and red (needs-you is at 38.4° light, 47.6° dark). Neighbouring hues alternate two tiers, A lighter and softer, B deeper and richer, so neighbours differ in lightness and chroma as well as hue. Same coverage as 4a: ground (the toolbar), pane (sidebar, tab strips, right pane), chatGround and toolOutputFill take the colour; the console, cards, fields, the document's page, the selected row, text and needs-you never change. None stays the first window's colour.

A new window takes the first colour in this list that no open window has (DL-312), so two open windows never share one until all twelve are in use; then it goes round again. How it is derived, and how to add more Hue of colour i = 92° + 22.5° × i, for i = 0 to 11. Tier = A when i is even, B when odd. Each surface takes the tier's L and C at that hue. Every value is inside sRGB, and the chroma is capped by the narrowest hue, teal. To add more, keep the recipe and put new hues halfway between the old ones (92° + 11.25° + 22.5° × i), giving 24 on the same arc, and give them a third tier between A and B. Then re-run the contrast check: docs/design/multi-window-studies/palette/window_palette.py.
