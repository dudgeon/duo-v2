What changes from DL-266 / DL-301 [P]

Added: Comment (⌥⌘M), after the writing marks. At the right, before the mic, the mode menu, labelled with the mode: Editing ⌄ or Suggesting ⌄ (Word says Reviewing; Google Docs and Notion say Suggesting).

The menu: Editing (edits change the text) or Suggesting (⇧⌘E; everyone's edits become suggestions, DL-237), checked. Then Next Change, Previous Change, Accept All, Reject All (and per person), Markup ▸ (All, Simple, None, Original), Show Closed Comments with its count, then the counts.

Retired: the 44-high review bar (MarkdownReviewBar) and its ‹ ›: amends DL-237's bar and Q-265. Every menu item and chord stays. Reverses DL-237's “not chosen: 2B/2C, an Editing/Suggesting/Viewing chip”: now it's a menu in the bar, without Viewing (a Duo document has no view-only mode).

How others do it

Google Docs: a mode menu at the top right, Editing / Suggesting / Viewing, labelled with the mode; Add comment in the toolbar.

Word for the web: Editing / Reviewing / Viewing at the top right beside Share; turning tracking on switches it to Reviewing.

Notion: ••• › Suggest edits; a “Suggesting” label then sits at the top until you stop it; Comment on the selection.

When it shows

While the document has the keyboard (DL-266), and whenever it has suggestions or comments or is Suggesting, so a tracked document never edits silently. Laid over the editor as built: 24, top padding 24, a 24 scroll margin.
