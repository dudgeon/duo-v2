All projects when there are many projects

Claude has run in dozens of folders on a normal Mac: every repo, Desktop, Downloads, temporary folders. Today's map (S2-2, DL-100) gives each one a full tile, so the few projects in Home sink under sixty folders nobody chose. Three asks: Home's projects come first, sort by recent or A–Z, and a card for Home itself on the map, because Home will collect many sessions and its pane only shows the open ones.

Today, at this scale

S2-2 as built, with 64 folders outside Home. Home's projects are fine in the first screen, but the map runs to several thousand points of folder tiles that all look equally important (cut short here). Home has no card; its 42 sessions are reachable only from the ★ heading. There is no order a user can choose, and nothing to filter by name.

Option A · Tiles for Home, rows for everything else (recommended)

Hierarchy

  • Home's projects keep their full tiles and columns, unchanged from S2-2.
  • Outside Home, each folder becomes one 24-point row: name, session count, last active. Rows sit under their parent-folder heading (DL-83 still holds), three groups across.
  • A group shows its 5 most recent rows, then + 18 more, which opens the group in place.
  • Attention can't hide: a folder outside Home with a session that needs you or is working is drawn as a full tile, in an ACTIVE OUTSIDE HOME row above the lists, until it goes idle.

Sort and find

  • A map header: Filter folders on the left, a sort popup on the right: Recent or Name.
  • The sort applies to tiles in each column, rows in each group, and the order of the groups. Home's unlabelled column and Home's card always come first.
  • Recent means the newest session activity in the folder, not the folder's own date.
  • The order settles when you arrive at All projects and doesn't shuffle while you look; a tile that becomes live outside Home moves up only when you next arrive.
  • The filter narrows tiles and rows by name or path as you type; Esc clears it.

Home's card

  • A full-width band at the top of the map: ★ claude-home, the goal from HOME.md, then Home's sessions in two columns, in attention order (S2-1's order), 8 at most.
  • 42 sessions · Open › at the right opens Home as a project, like the pane's ★ heading.
  • A session row opens that session in Home's pane on the left, staying at All projects; sessions already open there carry the activeTint.

Option A · details: the sort menu, Name order, a filter

Left: the sort popup open, with the same choice in View › Sort Projects By. Right: Name order with pay typed in the filter: tiles and rows that don't match go, groups with nothing left go, and the counts read 3 of 70.

Option B · Keep the tiles, fold what's outside Home

Hierarchy

  • Everything stays a tile, as S2-2 draws it.
  • Outside Home shows only the 6 most recent folders (live ones first), then a full-width Show all 64 folders outside Home, which unfolds the rest in place and remembers that it's open.
  • Lightest change to what's built; least dense when unfolded.

Sort

  • A two-part control at the top right of the map: Recent | A–Z. Always visible, one click, no menu.
  • No filter field: search (⇧⌘A) is the way to find a folder by name.

Home's card

  • Home is the first tile in its unlabelled column, the same width as the others, marked ★ and drawn with a text border so it reads first.
  • It lists 5 sessions, then 37 more ›, which opens Home as a project.

Option C · Two scopes: Home, and Everywhere

Hierarchy

  • A scope control at the top of the map: Home and Everywhere · 64. Duo opens on Home.
  • Home draws Home's card and Home's tiles, plus ACTIVE OUTSIDE HOME for live folders elsewhere, so nothing that needs you is out of sight.
  • Everywhere is a table of every folder outside Home, grouped by parent folder: name, sessions, last active, project file.
  • The strongest separation; but outside folders are a click away, which suits people who live in Home and hurts people who don't have one yet.

Sort

  • In Everywhere, click a column heading to sort by it; click again to reverse. The sorted heading turns text with a chevron.
  • In Home, the same sort popup as A.

Home's card

  • A compact band: the name, the goal, and counts by state (1 needs you · 1 working · 40 idle), with Open ›. The sessions themselves stay in the pane and the project.

Home's card, three ways

Any of these can go with any of A, B or C. A shows the band, B the tile, C the summary.
