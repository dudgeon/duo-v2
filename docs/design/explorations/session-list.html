Making sense of sessions

What Payments, checkout and "New session" each are today, what the sections of a project's session list mean, and options to make both obvious. Pick one option per question (click a card); "Copy my picks" gives you text to paste back to Claude.

The three layers, today

TopicA folder that holds projectsworkspace/payments/ is the Payments column on the map. It has no file of its own and you can't open it; it only groups tiles.
ProjectA folder with a PROJECT.mdworkspace/payments/checkout/ is the checkout tile. It has a goal, files and sessions; clicking it opens it.
SessionOne Claude Code conversationNew session inside checkout is one. It's called "New session" until Claude gives it a title after your first message.

Two more things fold sessions together: a group (sessions you put together by hand) and a thread (a session and its forks). Folders where Claude ran without a PROJECT.md show up under Elsewhere.

Older

Archived
Sessions you filed away (new today): out of the lists, still searchable.

What makes it confusing

  • "Idle" mixes the sessions you have open with ones you closed days ago.
  • The section words are Claude's states, not the question you're asking ("what am I in the middle of?").
  • Several sessions are called "New session" until Claude titles them, so they look alike.
  • On the map, nothing says that a column is a folder of projects and a tile is a project.
