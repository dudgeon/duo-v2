B. Every tab at its natural width in a strip that scrolls (trackpad, or a drag held at an edge). The selected tab scrolls into view whenever it changes. Tabs fade out at the edges, so it shows that there's more; one menu at the end lists every tab, the selected one checked.
For. A true spatial order: tabs never jump between slots, and you can scroll to see them all.
Against. Half-cut tabs at each edge, and where you are is a scroll position, not a count. Scrolling sideways over a terminal's strip is easy to do by accident. More motion than Duo uses elsewhere (DL-130). The hidden needs-you tab is shown only on the menu's count.
