B4 · Timing, placement, dismissal, keyboard

The rules for B1 and B3 (hover). B2 opens on a click and only shares placement and dismissal. These follow WCAG 1.4.13: the Explainer can be dismissed, hovered and stays put. The plain tag is the system's own help tag.

Opens with the system popover fade. No motion of Duo’s own; Reduce Motion: instant. A second Explainer nearby opens without the wait while one is open (Radix’s 300 ms skip).
Placement · default
Explainer

Above the button, left edges on the pane’s 16 inset, arrow on the button’s centre. It may overhang the next pane by up to 16.

Placement · no room above
Explainer

Below it, right edges aligned when the trigger sits at the right (Chat’s pill, C1). It never covers its own trigger.

Dismissal
Pointer leavesBoth button and Explainer, then 300 ms.
EscCloses it without moving the pointer.
Any clickCloses it. A click on the button also does the action.
Typing, scrolling, the window losing focusCloses it at once.
Keyboard and VoiceOver

Help › Explain Tasks (and Explain Chat Mode, Explain Home) opens the Explainer at its trigger with focus on Learn more; Esc closes it and focus goes back where it was. No new shortcut: the map is locked (DL-34).

VoiceOver: the help tag’s sentence is the button’s hint. A custom action, Explain, reads the title, the line and the points. The picture is decorative and hidden from VoiceOver.

Until dismissed (Geoff, 2026-10-12)

Don’t show again in the Explainer’s footer stops it opening on hover for good. The help tag stays, and Help › Explain Tasks still opens it. Until then it opens on every long hover.

Settings › General › Show dismissed explanations again brings back every one you dismissed. Office and Adobe both ship an off switch, and Adobe’s users asked for one loudly.
