How it appears
1Timing as B1 (B4): help tag “Track a new task in this project” at about 1 s; the Explainer 1.5 s later if the pointer stays still; hoverable; closes on leave (300 ms), Esc or a click. Here on A3’s New task row (and the fold header’s hover +).

2Numbers, not icons, tie each line to its part. They are the only filled marks, in text (never the accent).
3Lines are shorter than B1’s, since the picture carries the meaning: 34–60 characters each.
Trade-off
For: the fastest to scan, and it teaches what a task looks like in the sidebar and on the board. Against: the numbered marks are a new element, and the picture has to stay true to the real task card as that changes.
