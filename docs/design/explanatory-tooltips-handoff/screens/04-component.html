Parts
0Frame: 300 wide, pane, 1 rule, radiusPopover 10, shadowPopover; padding 14 16 12; 10 between parts. An arrow 12 on the trigger.
1Title: the feature’s noun, as Duo names it. title 14/20.
2Line: what it is, 60 characters at most. body 13/20.
3Picture: on ground, radius 6, drawn from Duo’s own parts at small size, in the tokens. Decorative: hidden from VoiceOver. No video, no motion, no accent.
4Points: two or three; a lead-in of 2–5 words in text, the rest in text2, 12/17; 90 characters each at most; a 14 stroke icon or a number tied to the picture.
5Footer: Learn more (text, underlined on hover; never the accent) and the Esc hint in text2.
Rules
  • Never the only place a fact lives; never something you must read to get on.
  • No buttons but Learn more; no fields.
  • Never on a disabled control, and never while a sheet or menu is open.
  • One at a time.
  • Until you’ve used the feature; then help tag only.
First three
  • Tasks on + New task (B).
  • Chat mode on the Terminal | Chat pill (C1).
  • Home on Home’s title (C2).
More only by decision: each one is a promise to keep its picture true.
