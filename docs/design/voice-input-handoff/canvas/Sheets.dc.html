The first press, and when it’s off [P]

Duo’s own sheet (SheetCenter) comes first, so the system’s question never arrives unexplained (F-54). It shows once per Mac; afterwards macOS remembers the answer.

1 · First press of the mic, or ⇧⌘D
Use your voice in Duo?
Duo turns what you say into text, on this Mac, using Apple’s speech recognition. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
macOS will ask whether Duo may use the microphone.
You can change this later in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Continue asks macOS (the microphone; Speech Recognition only if the fallback is needed) and starts listening if allowed. Not Now asks nothing; the next press shows this sheet again.

2 · Then macOS asks (drawn by the system)
“Duo” would like to access the microphone.
Duo turns what you say into text on this Mac when you press the mic. Nothing is recorded.
Don’t AllowAllow

Our part is the usage string, NSMicrophoneUsageDescription. The fallback’s, NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription: “Duo turns what you say into text on this Mac. Nothing leaves it.”

3 · What macOS can say, and what Duo shows
Not asked yet · sheet 1 on the next press.
Denied (you said no, or turned it off) · “Duo can’t use the microphone.” with Open Privacy Settings, in the hint row or the console bar.
Restricted (a profile, on a managed Mac) · the mic dashed, “Your Mac’s administrator doesn’t allow voice input for Duo.” No button.
Speech model can’t be fetched, or no model for your language · falls back to on-device SFSpeechRecognizer; if that has none either: “Voice input isn’t available in ‹language› on this Mac.”
Dictation turned off by profile · not known yet whether this stops Apple’s recognizer (Q-180). If it does, Duo shows it as restricted.
Scripted or isolated Duo · never asks; duo2 voice start answers “the microphone isn’t available in an isolated Duo”.
