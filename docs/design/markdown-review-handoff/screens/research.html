Resolve and close
✓ on the card resolves; ⋮ holds Edit, Delete, Link. A resolved thread leaves the margin; Comment history lists them, with Re-open.
✓ resolves (filled blue when resolved). Resolved threads hide unless the filter's "Show resolved" is on; a reply ("Reply to reopen…") or the ✓ reopens.
Two verbs, Geoff's: Close and Keep and Delete, as text buttons (Duo has one button). Closed threads hide; Markup › Show Closed Comments shows them; a reply reopens.
