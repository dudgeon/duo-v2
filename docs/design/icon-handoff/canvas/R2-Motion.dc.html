Two panes in motion

Fourteen loops built only from the icon's own parts, for launch, About, the website, onboarding or a loading state. [P] all. Inside the app, Reduce Motion would show the still icon.
1 Talk, then work
The prompt blinks, the console types, and the page fills.
2 Opens beside
The document slides out from behind the conversation.
3 Handoff
A line leaves the conversation and lands on the page as Claude's highlight.
4 At prompt
The chevron leans in and out; the page breathes. An idle or loading loop.
5 All projects
⌘↑ as motion: one window becomes four, then zooms back in.
6 Whoever you're talking to
The dark pane moves to the other side, as it does between Home and a project.
7 Every session kept
Earlier sessions fan out from behind the one you're in, then tuck back.
8 Comes together
The squircle grows, the window drops in with a little overshoot, and the detail follows.
9 Make room
The divider drags left and right; the page follows it.
10 Claude's additions
Each new line arrives in Duo's highlight, which fades, as it does in the editor.
11 Two sides
The window turns like a card: talk on one face, work on the other.
12 Launch
A Dock bounce with squash and stretch; the running dot appears when it lands.
13 Glass light
A soft sheen crosses the squircle, macOS 26 style. Good for the website or About.
14 Pick up where you left off
The window tucks into a small tab, then comes back exactly as it was.
