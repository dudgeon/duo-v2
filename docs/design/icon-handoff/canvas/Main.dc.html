LEGACY DUO
A hand-drawn coral loop on cream. One line that never ends, making two rounds: "duo", two things in one continuous line.

Duo v2 needs an icon

WHAT DUO V2 IS
Claude beside the work: the conversation in the middle, the document it writes on the right. Projects are plain folders, Home sits above them, and every session is kept so you can come back to it.
WHY NOT ORANGE
In Duo v2 orange means one thing: a session needs you. An orange icon in the Dock would read as an alert all day. None of these use it, and none use Claude's clay either.
WORTH KEEPING
The pair: two things that belong together (you and Claude, talk and work). The calm. Directions 02 and 12 keep the continuous line; the rest try new ground.
LEAVE BEHIND
The coral and cream, the hand-drawn wobble, and a loose mark that floats in a flat square: macOS 26 icons fill the squircle and carry depth.
How to read the boards. Twelve directions, each a different idea, form and palette. Each board shows the icon large, at 128, 64, 32 and 16 px, in Finder rows and in a Dock, light and dark. Every one is a proposal [P]. Comment on any board, or pick up to three to refine.
