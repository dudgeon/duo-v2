# App icon handoff (DL-131)

Duo v2's app icon: **2A′, "Refined, full bleed"** [G]. The squircle is the window: Duo's dark console on the left, a white page on the right, with a shadowed seam between them and Claude's highlight on the page. No orange: in Duo, orange means "needs you".

Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/LD3AaGRpx2JzDkRqpnLvXL (pages: Round 1, Round 2, Motion). `canvas/` is a snapshot of its boards (`.dc.html` sources and `canvas.json`).

## How it was chosen

1. **Round 1**: twelve directions, each a different idea, form and palette: two panes, one line, monogram, talk and page, pair, character, folder with a prompt, wordmark, glass, minimal line, constellation, and two links. Geoff picked **01 Two panes** alone.
2. **Round 2**: eight variants of 01 (2A refined, 2B full-bleed split, 2C two cards, 2D glass, 2E dark with a cursor, 2F navy, 2G pine, 2H three panes), compared in Finder and the Dock. Geoff then asked for "2A but full bleed", which became 2A′.
3. **Choice**: 2A′, with both sides centred vertically on the icon's middle [G].
4. **Motion**: fourteen animations of 01, kept as reference (ENH-18), not built.

## The drawing (1024 grid)

- **Squircle**: 824 × 824 at (100, 100), corner radius 185, with a drop shadow (y 10, blur 20, black 28%) and a 6 px edge at black 12%, so the white side keeps its shape on white.
- **Console** x 100–472: a top-to-bottom gradient, `#24272d` to `#121418` (the `console` token's family). **Page** x 472–924: `#ffffff` to `#e9edf1`. **Seam**: 6 px of black at 18% at x 472.
- **Full art (128 pt and up)**: the chevron (206,364)–(270,416)–(206,468) at 30 px in `consoleText`; three console lines in `consoleText2`; on the page, a heading in `text`, lines in `rule`, and a highlight block `#dfe3e8` holding two `controlEdge` lines. Each side's content is centred on y 512.
- **Small art (16 and 32 pt, at 1x and 2x)**: the chevron at 66 px, and three bars on the page. No console lines and no highlight, so it stays legible.
- macOS 26 and later draws the icon with its own rim light. It isn't put in a grey box (F-142). There are no dark or tinted variants yet (Q-81).
