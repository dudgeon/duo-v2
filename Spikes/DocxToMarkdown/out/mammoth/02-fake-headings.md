__Volunteer Handbook__

Welcome to the imaginary volunteer programme\. This handbook explains how shifts work\.

__Getting started__

Sign up on the shared calendar and pick a shift that suits you\.

__Your first shift__

Arrive ten minutes early\. A lead will show you around\.

__What to bring__

Gloves, water and a hat\.

__Safety__

Report any injury to the lead at once\.

__Tools__

Return every tool to the shed\.

__Never use the mower without a lead present, even if you have used one before\.__

*“Every hour helps\.”*
